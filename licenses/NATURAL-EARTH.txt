Natural Earth map data
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/

Natural Earth places its vector and raster map data in the public domain.
Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Waarp includes a simplified local world outline and regional label anchors.
The data identifies regions, not the physical locations of individual servers.
There are no remote tiles, geocoding requests, or bundled server locations.
